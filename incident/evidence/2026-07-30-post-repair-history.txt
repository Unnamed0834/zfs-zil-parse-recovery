`zpool history -il` output. It is not a complete raw history dump. Detailed
dataset paths, usernames, and device identifiers are excluded because they are
not needed for this evidence. Hostnames and the pool GUID are retained because
they are already published throughout the incident record.

scrub result are retained because they establish the recovery sequence.
