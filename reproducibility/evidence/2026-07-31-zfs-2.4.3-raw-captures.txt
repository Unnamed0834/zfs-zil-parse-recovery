    Two things follow, and both matter:

    - The hole read RETURNS rather than blocking. This is the empirical half
      of claim 4.9's conclusion.
    - It lands in `vdev_mirror_io_start()`, NOT in `vdev_missing_io_start()`.
      Claim 4.9 originally cited the latter. A read by DVA has `io_vd == NULL`
      and is dispatched through `vdev_mirror_ops`; the hole's own ops are never
      reached. The same frame appears in the kernel panic trace in section 3,
      so this is not a userspace-only artifact.

    Caveat: `zdb` runs in userspace libzpool with assertions enabled, so the
    ASSERT firing is a debug-build artifact, not the kernel's behaviour. What
    transfers is the entry point and the fact that the read does not block.
    The kernel-side confirmation is the successful import in
    2026-07-31-precondition-synthesis.txt.
